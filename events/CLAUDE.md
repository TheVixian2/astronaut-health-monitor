# events — CLAUDE.md

**Shared folder. Everyone writes here**, like `/presentation` — not a lane.
**Work on `main`** (coordination rule 3b): pull, small commit, push.

## What goes here

Anything said to us *by someone outside the team* during the event:

- judge and mentor visits — what they asked, what they suggested, what they
  seemed unimpressed by
- organiser announcements, rule clarifications, deadline changes
- other teams' questions that revealed a hole in our pitch

## Why it's separate from `presentation/ideas.md`

`ideas.md` is **our** thinking. This is **their** reaction. Keep them apart: when
a judge asks the same question twice, that's a signal, and it's invisible if it's
mixed in with our own notes.

## The rule

**Write it down within ten minutes of them walking away.** You will not remember
the wording by tonight, and the wording is the useful part — "how do you know
it's not just a fever?" tells you more than "they asked about accuracy".

Capture it even if you disagree. Especially if you disagree.

## Format

One file per visit: `YYYY-MM-DD-<who>.md`. Copy `_template.md`.

Every point gets a **decision**: actioned, rejected with a reason, or open. A
judge's suggestion that nobody decided on is worse than one you consciously
ignored — it just sits there making everyone uneasy.

## Open items

Anything still `open` at the end of a session should be mirrored into
`STATUS.md` under the relevant lane, or it won't get done.

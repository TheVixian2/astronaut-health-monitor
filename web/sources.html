Where the data comes from

Every number in Dr. Galactic is one of these. Full list with code locations: docs/nasa-data.md.

LIVE NASA DATAReal, right now

  • ISS cabin air: CO₂ (US Lab and Node 3), O₂, cabin pressure and temperature, live from NASA's public ISS Live telemetry. It is the cabin our simulated crew lives in (Commander → Habitat).
  • Earth–Mars light time for today from the NASA JPL Horizons API.

NASA STANDARD / FINDINGBuilt into the logic

  • CO₂ limit 3 mmHg (1-hour average), headaches from ~2.8 mmHg: NASA-STD-3001, OCHMO-TB-004.
  • Objective, minimally obtrusive monitoring against a personal pre-flight baseline for missions beyond 6 months; a private hygiene compartment, so the commander never sees who is in it (only hours per person; Mission Control sees who, for base operations): NASA-STD-3001, OCHMO-TB-016.
  • Station lighting modes 4500 K / 2700 K / 6500 K for the sleep and body-clock feature: ISS Solid-State Lighting Assembly.
  • NASA hazard and Human Research Program risk on every flag: 5 Hazards · Risks of Human Spaceflight.
  • 3-minute reaction test (PVT-B, a lapse = over 355 ms), one of NASA's behavioral-health standard measures.
  • Mood declines over time in isolation; no reported clinical depression (NASA HERA and SIRIUS crews): NASA HRP Investigators' Workshop 2026. Shapes a2's simulated decline; why we screen and never diagnose.
  • Reaction performance stays stable over 6-month ISS missions in healthy astronauts: Dev et al. 2024.
  • Crew of four, 850-day Mars mission: NASA Mars transit crew operations.

SIMULATEDStands in for hardware we don't have

  • Watch readings (heart rate, HRV, activity) come from the watchOS Simulator: there is no physical watch.
  • Other vitals and six weeks of history for all four crew members, around each person's own baseline.
  • Room positions from a fixed daily schedule, standing in for a Bluetooth beacon per module.
  • Check-in answers (PHQ-2, mood, reaction test) seeded so the demo is identical every run.

Why not real astronaut data? Astronaut medical and behavioral records are protected health data. NASA collects exactly these measures (HERA Standard Measures: PANAS mood, GHQ) through the NASA Life Sciences Portal, by request only. NASA has this data; this tool would feed it.

REAL + SYNTHETICDemo CSVs and our model test

  • Sample CSV (Upload CSV on each console): 63 real days of one person's Fitbit Sense data from LifeSnaps (Yfantidou et al., Scientific Data 2022, CC BY 4.0): resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, activity, SpO₂, breathing and mood. About 85-90 % of values are real; gaps and core temperature are synthetic and named in the file's filled column.
  • Can a watch predict mood? We trained XGBoost on 21 LifeSnaps people: it did no better than each person's own average mood. That is why the score pairs watch signals with a validated questionnaire.

BACKGROUNDCited in the pitch

  • Morale dips in the third quarter of a mission: Stuster 2010, ISS astronaut journals.
  • Behavioral risk rises with mission length: NASA Behavioral Medicine Evidence Report 2025.
  • Support from crewmates linked to lower evening stress: Bell et al. 2025.
  • Astronaut sleep in flight: Barger et al. 2014.

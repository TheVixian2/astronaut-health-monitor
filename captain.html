Concept layout of a Mars transit habitat. Each module has a Bluetooth beacon the watch detects. Who is in the hygiene module is shown only to Mission Control; you see hours per person. Live positions are for safety (fire, depressurisation).

Time with crewmates is one input of the wellbeing profile (12 % of the score; the method is at GET /wellbeing/method). The evidence for it is limited (self-reported support linked to lower stress in NASA analog crews).

Crew warnings are decision support, not a diagnosis. Keep psychological information confidential and raise it in private.
